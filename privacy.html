Privacy Policy

Brand: QEVORA
Last Updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QEVORA may collect, use, protect and retain information submitted through its website and related services.

QEVORA should only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the specific service being provided and should handle that information in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the service and transaction, information may include:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Transaction and trade information.
  • Selected payout method.
  • Bank details needed to process an approved payout.
  • Cryptocurrency wallet information where applicable.
  • Uploaded supporting documents or images.
  • Technical information such as browser, device and website activity.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To process and review submitted transactions.
  • To verify transaction information and prevent fraud.
  • To communicate with users about their transactions.
  • To maintain and improve the QEVORA platform.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Information Security

QEVORA should use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal and transaction information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore avoid sharing passwords, private keys or other credentials through ordinary forms or unofficial support channels.

4. Information Sharing

QEVORA should not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the service, process a transaction, prevent fraud, or satisfy a legal obligation.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for transaction records, customer support, fraud prevention, legal compliance and other legitimate business purposes.

6. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of their personal information.

Requests concerning personal information should be directed to QEVORA through the official support channel.

7. Third-Party Services

QEVORA may use third-party services for hosting, payments, analytics, communications or other operational functions. Those services may have their own privacy policies and security practices.

8. Policy Updates

QEVORA may update this Privacy Policy when its services, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact & Support

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact QEVORA through the official support channel published on the QEVORA website.
